PsihoApp is scheduling and client-management software used by independent therapy and psychology practices to run their day-to-day work — sessions, groups, and payments.

Plain-language summary: we store the account details you sign in with, and the client, session, and payment records your practice enters. We don't sell any of it. Practice data is kept separate, practice by practice. Full detail below.
01

Scope

This policy covers PsihoApp's web application and the accounts created within it. It applies to two kinds of people: practitioners who sign up to run their practice on PsihoApp, and clients whose contact and appointment details a practitioner enters into their own practice's records.

If your practice is subject to health-data regulation in your jurisdiction (for example GDPR's special-category data, or a local equivalent of HIPAA), you remain the data controller for the client records you enter — PsihoApp acts as your processor. Speak with your own counsel about what that means for your intake and consent process.

02

Data we collect

We collect only what the app needs to schedule sessions, manage clients, and let you sign in.

CategoryWhat's includedWhere it comes from
Account data Name, email address, and — if you sign in with Google — the name and email your Google account shares with us. You, at sign-up
Practice data Your practice name, and the roles of anyone you invite to it (owner or member). You, when setting up or inviting
Client records Name, phone number, and email address of the clients you add to your practice. You, entered manually
Session & payment records Appointment times, session price, payment status and method, and group membership. You, entered manually
Technical data Basic request logs (IP address, timestamps) kept for security and troubleshooting. Generated automatically

We do not collect payment card numbers — PsihoApp only records that a payment happened, not how to charge one.

03

How we use it

  • To run the core product: showing your client list, calendar, and payment history.
  • To send session-related emails — confirmations, changes, and cancellations — to the client email addresses you enter.
  • To authenticate you and keep your practice's data separate from every other practice on PsihoApp.
  • To keep the service secure and to diagnose problems when something breaks.

We do not use client records for advertising, and we do not sell personal data to anyone, ever.

04

Legal basis

Where GDPR or a similar framework applies: we process account data under contract (to provide the service you signed up for), and practices process their own client data under whatever legal basis they establish with their clients — typically consent or a service agreement between the practitioner and the client. PsihoApp itself acts as a processor for that client data, not the controller.

05

06

Retention

We keep account and practice data for as long as your account is active. Client and session records are kept for as long as your practice keeps them in the app — you can edit or delete individual client and session entries at any time from the Admin Panel. If you close your account, we delete your practice's data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain it for legal or accounting reasons.

07

Security

  • Tenant isolation — every practice's clients, sessions, and payments are scoped to that practice and never visible to another practice.
  • Encrypted transport — all traffic between your browser and PsihoApp is sent over HTTPS.
  • Scoped invites — team invite links are signed and expire after seven days, so a practice can only be joined by someone holding a link the owner actually sent.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe you've found a vulnerability, please contact us using the details below before disclosing it publicly.

08

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data we hold about you. Practitioners can self-serve most of this from the Admin Panel. If you're a client of a PsihoApp practice and want to exercise these rights over your own records, please contact your practitioner directly — they control that data. For anything involving your own account data, contact us using the details below.

09

Children

PsihoApp accounts are intended for practitioners aged 18 and over. Client records may of course belong to minors, entered and managed on their behalf by the practitioner responsible for their care.

10

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify practice owners by email.
